*gaffer — audio-reactive simulated lens on a depth map for Resolume (FFGL) + OpenFX; LOCAL ONLY, never pushed, never loaded into any host*

**gaffer** (started 2026-08-23) — a simulated lens on a depth map with the music
holding it. `~/projects/resolume/gaffer`, FFGL id **`GF01`**, OFX
`com.stoatworks.gaffer`. **Local git only — no GitHub remote, nothing pushed,
no release, no website entry, no demo deployed.** MIT intended, public intended.

**Two halves, one machine.** The *rattle*: a camera in front of a PA is a damped
mass on a spring and answers at ITS frequency, so four oscillators (pan/tilt/
roll/lens) at incommensurate frequencies, driven by the **change** in the bass
envelope — a kick rings it, a drone does not move it. The *rack*: Off / Follow /
Pull / Sweep / Stutter, cued off the transport, a button or the kick.

**The one idea.** The thin-lens equation says the circle of confusion is linear
in the difference of RECIPROCAL depths, i.e. in disparity — what a depth map
actually holds. `coc = K(d - focus)`, and `K` and the frame scale share the
denominator `1 - phi*focus`, so **depth of field collapsing up close and focus
breathing are the same fact**. phi < 1 by construction: no divide-by-zero guard,
same discipline as [vertigo](https://github.com/stoatworks-labs/vertigo/blob/main/docs/NOTES.md) (`vertigo`).

⚠️ **Every weight in the gather is an area over an area — do not tune one.** A
first version weighted loop taps with no area measure and the look changed with
the Quality control (a sharp subject 77% itself at 96 taps, 96% at 12). Layers
are split against the **receiving pixel's own surface**, not the focal plane;
`sameW` clamped to 1 IS that surface's opacity.

⚠️ **Highlight is a power MEAN, not a per-sample weighting.** Weighting
normalises by the sum of the weights, so at a strong setting the answer is
whichever tap was brightest: on a checkerboard it drew the sampling pattern, got
SHARPER as the blur grew, and did not improve from 32 taps to 128 (variance in
the estimator, not noise). Exactly 1 at zero, and both builds branch on that.

⚠️ **The iris polygon is evaluated at `ang + pi` and the SAME value used in the
coverage test.** Test against the circular `rad` instead and every corner is
placed then immediately rejected — a hexagon setting silently renders a circle.
Caught only by the Fourier iris test. Blades are area-normalised.

⚠️ **`half` is a GLSL reserved word** and cost the first compile. Diag.cpp earned
its keep on day one.

Five depth sources: Radial / Luma / Alpha / **Split H / Split V**. The Split
pair is the only one where picture and depth are independent, which is why the
harness measures the lens through it — an impulse through a KNOWN depth.

**Verified offline only, ~8 s:** `tools/verify.sh`. `--focus`/`--rattle`/
`--presets` need no GPU. Bokeh measured as an impulse response — radius vs
Lens.cpp over 48 combinations, x and y separately, light conserved at 1.0000 of
the pinhole. Iris symmetry with the **tap-pattern baseline subtracted as a
vector** (the golden-angle spiral's own 8-fold signature is as strong as an
octagon's). Both nulls 0 of 921,600 bytes, in FFGL and under ofxprobe. Stateless
vs stateful rack agree to 2.8e-17 — that is what keeps FFGL and OpenFX rendering
the same rack. All 30 controls live.

**Never loaded into Resolume or Resolve. No audio has ever reached it, no
transport, no real depth pass, no Windows build, nothing timed.**

The demo carries the **OpenFX** parameter set (Tempo + Kick) because a browser
has no audio, and its shader is GENERATED by `demo/tools/sync_shaders.py` with
`check_shaders.py` still in verify. Its `_headers` is the only one in the fleet
whose `connect-src` allows the intake origin — see
[support footer](https://github.com/stoatworks-labs/fleet-notes/blob/main/notes/reference_support_footer.md); the kit master and every other Resolume demo
